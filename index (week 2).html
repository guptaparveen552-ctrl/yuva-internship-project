A modal dialog you can reuse anywhere

Three modals on this page share one component file. Each is defined by markup alone — no extra JavaScript per modal. Open them with a mouse, or tab to a button and press Enter, then try Tab, Shift + Tab and Escape inside the dialog.

Content modal

The plain case: a title, some body copy, one close action. Click the dimmed backdrop to dismiss it.

Form modal

Holds focusable fields, validates before closing, and reports its own errors instead of relying on the browser.

Confirm modal

A destructive action. Backdrop clicks are ignored here, so the choice has to be deliberate.

What is Pathlight?

Pathlight is a streak-based log for placement prep. You record one line per problem solved, project task finished, or mock interview taken, and the weekly view shows which patterns still need reps.

It is deliberately small: no leaderboards, no dashboard to configure before you can log your first entry.
